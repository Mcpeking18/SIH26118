What this measures. The badge is a passive lead-acetate dosimeter: it darkens in proportion to the H₂S it has absorbed, so a scan gives the cumulative exposure over the time it was worn. It cannot tell you the concentration right now, and one scan cannot recover a 15-minute STEL peak — for that, scan at the start and end of the period, or use a portable monitor.

If a reading looks wrong. Do not re-scan repeatedly to get a better number. Follow the on-screen instruction once; if it fails again, send the badge for lab analysis and record the worker as unmonitored for the shift.
